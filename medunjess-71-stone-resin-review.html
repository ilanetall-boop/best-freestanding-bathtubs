What is stone resin and how does it compare to acrylic?

Stone resin, also called solid surface, is a composite of natural minerals bound with resin. It holds heat longer than acrylic, feels warmer to the touch, and can be sanded to remove minor scratches instead of needing a full patch. The tradeoff is weight: a stone resin tub like the MEDUNJESS 71" is considerably heavier than an acrylic tub of the same size, so floor support is worth checking before you buy.

How much floor space does the MEDUNJESS 71" tub need?
