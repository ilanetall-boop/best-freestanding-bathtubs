Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Rough-in height matters more than looks: floor-mount faucets need roughly 18 to 24 inches of clearance and enough distance from the wall to clear the tub rim.
  • Floor-mount and deck-mount are different systems: floor-mount units run supply lines up through the floor, while deck-mount units connect through the tub deck or an adjacent wall.
  • Finish thickness varies by brand: budget units use a thin plated finish that can wear at contact points, while mid-range and premium models use finishes that hold color longer.
  • A separate handheld sprayer adds real function for rinsing kids, pets, or the tub itself, and most floor-mount kits on this list include one.
  • Valve type drives long-term reliability more than finish color does, so check whether a faucet uses a ceramic disc valve before comparing paint jobs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a floor-mount and deck-mount freestanding bathtub faucet?

A floor-mount faucet runs its supply lines up through the floor near the tub, while a deck-mount version connects through the tub's own deck or an adjacent wall ledge. Most true freestanding tubs use floor-mount hardware since they don't have a flat deck surface to mount to. All seven faucets in this comparison are floor-mount models for that reason.

How much does it cost to install a freestanding bathtub faucet?

Installation labor is typically separate from the faucet price and depends on whether supply lines already exist in the floor. Budget beyond the $119.99 to $2,389.00 range covered in this guide, since a new floor-mount rough-in usually adds several hundred dollars in plumbing work on top of the fixture cost.

Which is the best freestanding bathtub faucet for a budget remodel?

For a budget remodel, the Aolemi floor-mount faucet at $119.99 and the ARCORA brushed gold model at $195.69 both cover the basics without the premium pricing of the Delta Trinsic or WOODBRIDGE tub packages. Choose ARCORA for a specific finish and Aolemi if price is the only factor.
